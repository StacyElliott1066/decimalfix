    Important Route Planner Disclaimer

    This application is an aid for Supervisors of Flight (SOF) to help double-check a Solo Plan and identify items that may need additional review.

    It is not a weather planning tool, weather briefing system, dispatch release system, or substitute for approved aviation weather sources.

    The responsibility for weather planning, go/no-go decision making, and determining whether the flight can be conducted safely remains solely with the student pilot and the CFI who signed the student off.

    The SOF should use this only as a supplemental cross-check. Always verify weather, forecasts, restrictions, NOTAMs, aircraft limitations, route suitability, and applicable school/FAA requirements using approved sources and procedures.
